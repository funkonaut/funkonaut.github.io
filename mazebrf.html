Embossing with BrailleBlaster

  1. Press Download braille file.
  2. In BrailleBlaster, choose File, Open. The maze opens in Braille Preview.
  3. Choose File, Emboss (Control E) and pick your embosser.
  4. Set Interpoint to No.
  • Cells per line and lines per page must match your embosser.
  • If they are bigger, lines wrap and break the maze.
  • Emboss a test page first. If paths feel too narrow or wide, change the path width.

Embossing with BrailleBlaster

  1. Press Download braille file.
  2. In BrailleBlaster, choose File, Open. The puzzle opens in Braille Preview.
  3. Choose File, Emboss (Control E) and pick your embosser.
  4. Set Interpoint to No.
  • Cells per line and lines per page must match your embosser.
  • If they are bigger, lines wrap and break the puzzle.
  • Students read the word list first, then search each row for a word.
  • Every letter is uncontracted, so “the” is spelled t h e.

Tactile Topo Maker

Works with any braille embosser. Graphics embossers can also use QuickTac (.sig).

Feel contour lines, or feel the dots get thicker as the land gets higher.

It can also make a 3D printer model (.stl) of the same area.

    Embossing tips

    • Braille file: set your embosser to the same cells per line and lines per page.
    • Braille file: turn off translation and page numbers, or the picture will shift.
    • QuickTac: open the .sig file, check File, Page Setup, then emboss from QuickTac.
    • 3D model: print flat side down. It needs no supports.
    • Emboss on one side only, with interpoint off.
    • Emboss one test page first and check that the rows line up.
